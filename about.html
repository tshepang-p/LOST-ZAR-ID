Mission

Our mission is to reconnect lost identification documents with their rightful owners by providing a safe, accessible, and community-driven platform. We strive to reduce the stress and risks caused by lost IDs while promoting trust, responsibility, and convenience for everyone.

My Story

A very close of acquaintance of mine has asked me to collect his qualification certificate from the University. He was not able collect it himself as he did not live in the same town as the university he attended. Rather than fetching it himself, he asked me to collect it on his behalf. Some of the requirments that were needed for me to collect his documents was my ID Card. One day when I went to collect his documents, I accidentally lost my ID Card. After searching, I was unsuccessful with finding my ID Card. I had to make a temporary ID Document as I need to have an ID. Sitting on those silver chairs at home affairs. Paying costs that I did not plan for... making affidavits and all. I came up with this platform to help people who lost their ID Cards and ID books get their ID back. This platform serves as a point of contact for the person who found the ID , and the person who lost their ID cards.

It was frustrating and time-consuming to replace it. That's why I created this website - to help others like me who have faced the same problem.

With this platform, you can easily submit your lost ID details and help others find their lost IDs too.
